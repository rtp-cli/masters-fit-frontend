# Competitive Landscape Digest: Age-Focused, Low-Impact & Rehab-Adjacent Platforms vs. MastersFit
**Research date: 2026-07-30. All verification dates are 2026-07-30 unless noted. Labels: CONFIRMED (verified on primary source), REPORTED (secondary source), INFERRED (analyst judgment), UNAVAILABLE.**

---

## 1. Bold (agebold.com)

- **Business model:** B2B2C via Medicare Advantage plans. CONFIRMED — Bold's FAQ states it is "offered at no cost through select health plans," accessed via Renew Active (UnitedHealthcare) or One Pass (https://www.agebold.com/faq; https://www.agebold.com/partnerships). Positions itself to payers as "the leading prevention partner for Medicare Advantage," selling on care-cost reduction, Star Ratings, and member retention (https://www.agebold.com/partnerships).
- **DTC status (the question asked):** No paid DTC offering exists. CONFIRMED — the only consumer path outside a health plan is "Bold Basic, a free library of select exercise classes" (https://www.agebold.com/faq). No paid consumer tier is described anywhere on the site.
- **Pricing:** $0 to eligible plan members; Bold Basic free. CONFIRMED (same URL).
- **Demographic/positioning:** 65+ Medicare senior. CONFIRMED — FAQ: "If you're above 65, you're in the right place." Language is "healthy aging," "movement as medicine," fall/arthritis prevention.
- **Programming:** Assessment-driven personalized exercise programs + daily live classes + on-demand video library. CONFIRMED (FAQ: personalized programs based on goals/days-per-week, "daily live classes, and thousands of on-demand video classes"). Personalization is algorithmic/assessment-based, not an LLM-style AI coach. INFERRED.
- **Clinical claims (vendor-claimed):** "Proven to increase physical activity by 182%"; "8 in 10 members more likely to stay with their plan." REPORTED via Bold marketing pages surfaced in search (https://www.agebold.com/partnerships) — vendor-claimed, not independently verified.
- **Scale/funding:** $27M total: $7M seed led by Andreessen Horowitz (https://www.agebold.com/press/bold-announces-7-million-in-seed-funding), $17M Series A led by Rethink Impact, Sept 2023, with Samsung Next, Khosla (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bold-announces-17-million-series-a-to-further-accelerate-growth-with-medicare-plans-and-providers-and-support-the-healthy-aging-goals-of-older-adults-everywhere-301924948.html). CONFIRMED. Member counts UNAVAILABLE.
- **Vs. MastersFit:** Validates "assessment → personalized program for aging bodies" but in a payer channel and an older (65+) demographic. Weak direct competition for a 40+ DTC AI app; its deliberate avoidance of DTC (free Basic tier only) suggests the venture consensus is that the older-adult money is in payer contracts. INFERRED.

## 3. Vivo (teamvivo.com)

- **Business model:** DTC subscription (live classes), now adding a clinical/B2B evidence track. CONFIRMED.
- **Pricing (verified today at https://teamvivo.com/membership/):** $99/mo (1 class/wk), $159/mo (2/wk, "most popular"), $199/mo (3/wk); free 2-week trial, no credit card; includes equipment welcome kit + reassessments every 2 months. CONFIRMED.
- **Demographic/positioning:** 55+ (some marketing 53+). "This isn't a workout. It's a way forward" — independence, capability, anti-frailty. CONFIRMED (https://teamvivo.com/).
- **Programming:** Live small-group (max 12) Zoom classes led by certified trainers; 4 difficulty levels; human personalization, no AI. CONFIRMED.
- **Injury/clinical:** Trainers trained to modify for mobility limits. Running a 250-participant RCT with Intermountain Health (announced 2024-02-06) on knee osteoarthritis in 55+ adults — deliberately building clinical evidence to expand into chronic-condition channels (prediabetes, osteoporosis per CEO Eric Levitan). CONFIRMED (https://teamvivo.com/vivo-and-intermountain-health-partner-to-treat-osteoarthritis/).
- **Scale/funding:** UNAVAILABLE (no credible member/funding figures found).
- **Vs. MastersFit:** Adjacent, not head-on: sells human connection and accountability at 4–8x MastersFit-type pricing. Validates that 55+ consumers pay premium prices for strength training specifically. Its high-touch model can't scale down-market; an AI app is the cheap complement, not a substitute. Plausible partner (AI programming between live sessions). INFERRED.

## 4. Pvolve

- **Business model:** DTC — streaming subscription + franchise studios + equipment/bundles. CONFIRMED (https://www.pvolve.com/).
- **Pricing (verified today at https://help.pvolve.com/en-US/pvolve-streaming-membership-plans-and-pricing-monthly-and-annual-costs-477564):** $24.99/mo or $224.91/yr (≈$19.74/mo); 14-day free trial. CONFIRMED. 1,600+ on-demand classes + live virtual studio. REPORTED.
- **Demographic/positioning:** Women-centric functional fitness; not age-branded but heavily marketed to women 40+ / menopause ("Does it actually work for women 40+?" is its review ecosystem framing). REPORTED (https://www.herdailyfit.com/programs/pvolve/). Jennifer Aniston is its highest-profile backer/ambassador (widely reported; not re-verified today — REPORTED).
- **Programming:** Video library + live classes + proprietary resistance equipment. Program quizzes but no per-user AI personalization. INFERRED from site structure.
- **Clinical claims (vendor-claimed):** University of Exeter 12-week study, 72 women 40–60: +19% hip/lower-body strength, +21% flexibility, improved lipids in perimenopausal participants vs. standard activity guidelines (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240117660302/en/First-of-its-Kind-Study-from-University-of-Exeter-and-Pvolve-Researches-Impact-of-Exercise-on-Women-Pre-and-Post-Menopause). CONFIRMED the study exists and these are the announced findings; results are vendor-publicized, sponsor-funded — label vendor-claimed.
- **Scale/funding:** UNAVAILABLE in this pass (franchise count/revenue not verified).
- **Vs. MastersFit:** Strongest DTC evidence that the 40–60 demographic (at least women) is a venture-scale consumer fitness market, and that clinical studies are becoming a DTC marketing weapon. Competes for the same 40s–50s customer, but with content-library economics, women-first branding, and equipment lock-in — not AI strength programming for both sexes. INFERRED.

## 5. SilverSneakers GO (Tivity Health)

- **Business model:** B2B2C only — a free companion app to the SilverSneakers Medicare Advantage benefit; no DTC purchase path. CONFIRMED via eligibility rules: must be enrolled in an MA/Medigap plan that includes SilverSneakers (https://www.medicarefaq.com/faqs/silver-sneakers-program/; https://health.usnews.com/medicare/articles/what-is-silver-sneakers).
- **Pricing:** $0 to eligible members. CONFIRMED.
- **Demographic:** 65+ (or Medicare-eligible via disability). CONFIRMED.
- **Programming:** App = location finder, class schedules, on-demand workout videos, basic 4/8/12-week programs; plus live virtual classes and 15,000+ gym network through the core benefit. REPORTED.
- **Clinical/injury:** General senior-fitness levels; no individualized injury accommodation. INFERRED. Scale: ~18M eligible older adults REPORTED (https://www.getsetup.com/post/empowering-older-adults-getsetups-social-learning-revolution).
- **Key 2026 dynamic:** MA plans are cutting fitness benefits — "in 2026, the number of plans offering SilverSneakers declined as plans looked for ways to cut costs," some dropping fitness entirely (https://health.usnews.com/medicare/articles/what-is-silver-sneakers; https://wtop.com/news/2026/05/does-medicare-cover-silversneakers-costs-eligibility-and-plan-changes/). REPORTED. Implication: seniors losing free benefits may become paying DTC customers — a mild tailwind for DTC apps. INFERRED.
- **Vs. MastersFit:** Not a competitor for 40s–50s users (Medicare-gated). Sets the "free through my insurance" anchor MastersFit will hit at the 65+ edge of its funnel. INFERRED.

### Hinge/Sword collision assessment (asked directly)
- Both are racing to become multi-condition AI care platforms, but **distribution stays employer/health-plan** — their unit economics ($700–$2,000+ PMPM-equivalent case rates) don't translate to $10–30/mo consumer subscriptions, and neither shows DTC signals as of today. INFERRED from all sources above.
- **Real collision risk is indirect and 2–4 years out:** (a) Hinge's Fall Prevention and Sword's Move/wellness modules mean a 45-year-old MastersFit prospect with back pain may already get a free, clinically-supervised exercise app through work — free-benefit substitution, not head-to-head marketing. (b) Sword's Phoenix "AI specialist for everything" trajectory is the most plausible future consumer-brand pivot given its consumer-grade AI investment, especially post-IPO pressure. INFERRED. Watch items: any Hinge/Sword Medicare Advantage supplemental-benefit deals (both are moving there — Hinge fall prevention CONFIRMED) and any app-store consumer SKUs.

## Synthesis for MastersFit (all INFERRED, from the above)

1. **The 40–64 DTC lane is structurally underserved.** Every well-capitalized age-focused player (Bold, Nymbl, SilverSneakers, GetSetUp, Hinge, Sword, insurer benefits) monetizes 65+/covered lives through payers. The only true DTC comps for 40+ are Mighty Health (50+, human-coach, ~$29/mo, small and quiet) and Pvolve (women 40+, content library, $24.99/mo). Nobody found in this sweep pairs *AI-personalized strength programming* with a *40+ DTC* position.
2. **Pricing anchors:** $24.99–29/mo (Pvolve, Mighty) for app/content; $99–199/mo (Vivo) for live human training; $0 via insurance at 65+. A 40+ AI app has clear room in the $15–30/mo band with "smarter than a video library, 1/5 the price of live training" framing.
3. **Evidence is becoming table stakes even in DTC** (Pvolve/Exeter, Vivo/Intermountain RCT). A lightweight outcomes study or published cohort metrics would be a differentiating investment for MastersFit.
4. **Partnership > collision** with Bold/Nymbl/GetSetUp (older, payer-side); **watch** Sword (Phoenix multi-condition AI, post-Kaia consolidation) as the likeliest future consumer-channel entrant, and Hinge's employer-channel fall-prevention/wellness creep as free-substitution risk for injured 40+ prospects.
