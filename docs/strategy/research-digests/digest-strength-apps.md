# Competitive Research Digest — Strength-Training Apps vs MastersFit
**Verification date: 2026-07-30.** Labels: CONFIRMED (verified today on cited first-party source), REPORTED (third-party/estimate), INFERRED (analysis), UNAVAILABLE.

## 1. JEFIT
- Pricing CONFIRMED: Free tier (logging + 1,400+ exercises); Elite $12.99/mo or $69.99/yr; 7-day trial of "Adaptive Plans". https://apps.apple.com/us/app/jefit-workout-plan-gym-tracker/id449810000
- Rating CONFIRMED: 4.8, ~47K ratings (US).
- Programming: hybrid logger + templates; now markets "Adaptive AI Coach" / "AI-powered progressive overload" + muscle-recovery tracking. INFERRED: set/rep/load progression on routines, not whole-week regeneration.
- Injury/limitation accommodation: UNAVAILABLE (not mentioned). Age positioning: none. Claims "over 12 million people" (marketing claim).
- Wearables: Apple Watch live-sync, Apple Health. REPORTED review complaints: bugs, logouts, poor sync.
- vs MastersFit: big library/base, bolt-on AI, no injury awareness, no age-smart programming.

## 2. Strong
- Pricing CONFIRMED: Free (unlimited logging, 3 custom routines cap REPORTED); Pro $4.99/mo, $29.99/yr, $99.99 lifetime. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/strong-workout-tracker-gym-log/id464254577
- Rating CONFIRMED: 4.9, ~108K ratings.
- Pure logger/tracker. No generation, no AI, no adaptation. No injury accommodation. No age positioning.
- Wearables: Apple Watch live sync; Apple Health.
- Scale REPORTED: 3M+ downloads; ~19 employees, Singapore.
- vs MastersFit: not a programming competitor; the bar for logging UX (4.9/108K). MastersFit wins on "tell me what to do".

## 3. Dr. Muscle (closest positioning competitor)
- Pricing CONFIRMED: $48.99/mo (also $49.99 SKU) or $399.99/yr; meal add-on $18.99/mo; free trial no card; free plan degrades to one AI recommendation/day (https://dr-muscle.com/free-plan/). $359.99 lifetime REPORTED only. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dr-muscle-ai-personal-trainer/id1073943857
- Rating CONFIRMED: 4.5, only 377 ratings (US). REPORTED: Play "50,000+ downloads"; ~97K total (AppBrain); newsletter ~50.7K. INFERRED: small — ~1/700th Fitbod's ratings volume.
- Programming CONFIRMED: algorithmic generation updating after every set/session — rest-pause, automated deloads, daily undulating periodization; "built by an exercise scientist" (Carl Juneau PhD); claims "59% faster". INFERRED rules-based/ML, not LLM.
- Inputs: gym/home equipment, program level, auto deloads. Marketing references "25 or 55, recovering from an injury" (REPORTED); explicit injury mechanics UNAVAILABLE.
- Age positioning CONFIRMED: App Store copy appeals 35+ w/ testimonials 46–58; dedicated SEO pages for "best fitness apps 40+", "women over 40" — funnel-level, not product-level. Homepage has no explicit age claim.
- Wearables: Apple Health sync only.
- vs MastersFit: most direct positioning overlap; 4–7x higher price ($399/yr), tiny base, dated UX reputation (REPORTED). Proves willingness-to-pay at $400/yr in this demo.

## 4. JuggernautAI
- Pricing CONFIRMED: $34.99/mo or $349.99/yr, 2-week trial. https://www.juggernautai.app/pricing
- Rating CONFIRMED: 4.8, ~5.6K ratings.
- Programming CONFIRMED: algorithmic individualized periodization; adapts from logged results + daily readiness check-ins (sleep, nutrition, soreness) adjusting the day's session. Powerlifting/powerbuilding only. Not LLM (INFERRED).
- Inputs: frequency, experience, goals, equipment w/ substitutions, weak-point selection. Injury: substitution-level only; no age modeling.
- Age positioning: none — serious SBD lifters.
- vs MastersFit: gold standard for adaptive strength UX (readiness ratings ≈ what age-aware autoregulation should feel like) — study it; no audience overlap.

## 5. FitnessAI
- Pricing CONFIRMED (IAP ladder): $3.99/wk; $9.99–$19.99/mo; $59.99–$89.99/yr. No functional free tier. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fitness-ai-gym-workout-planner/id1446224156
- Rating CONFIRMED: 4.7, ~56K ratings.
- Programming: algorithm on "5.9M workouts" auto-adjusting sets/reps/weights; not LLM (INFERRED); per-exercise progression math, not weekly program design.
- RED FLAG CONFIRMED: last update July 23, 2024 — 2+ years stale. INFERRED zombie app.
- No injury accommodation, no age positioning. HealthKit sync (flaky per reviews).
- vs MastersFit: cautionary tale; its 56K ratings show demand for the promise.

## 6. Alpha Progression
- Pricing CONFIRMED: Pro $12.99/mo or $79.99/yr, 14-day trial; genuinely usable free tier (logging, 795 exercises) minus generator/recommendations. https://alphaprogression.com/en
- Rating CONFIRMED: 4.9, ~2.1K ratings (US). German-market heavy (REPORTED). Claims "25M+ workouts completed".
- Programming CONFIRMED: algorithmic plan generator (goal, experience, equipment, frequency, duration) + adaptive per-set recommendations. Deliberately not AI-marketed.
- Inputs: goals, multiple gym equipment profiles, frequency, duration, muscle emphasis, editable everything, auto warm-ups. Injury: UNAVAILABLE/none.
- Age positioning: none. Wearables: minimal.
- vs MastersFit: best pure value competitor ($79.99/yr real generator); hypertrophy-bro framing, no injury/age/recovery logic, weak US brand.

## 7. Boostcamp
- Pricing CONFIRMED: Free core (130+ expert programs — 5/3/1, nSuns, GZCLP, Reddit PPL; Helms, Nuckols — 10,000+ community programs, full tracking); Pro $14.99/mo (no trial) or $59.99/yr (7-day trial). https://www.boostcamp.app/pro
- Rating CONFIRMED: 4.8, ~9.6K ratings. Claims 1M+ lifters, 300M+ workouts (marketing).
- Programming: template library + tracking; Pro adds "personalized programs tuned to goal/schedule" and new "Create programs with AI" (depth UNAVAILABLE; INFERRED early LLM builder). No session-by-session auto-adaptation (INFERRED).
- Injury accommodation: none. Age positioning: none; Reddit-native intermediates.
- vs MastersFit: owns "proven free programs" — opposite thesis; user must self-manage; zero age/injury intelligence.

## Fitbod — Scale Benchmark
- CONFIRMED 2026-07-30: US App Store 4.8, ~279K ratings. IAP: Elite $12.99–$15.99/mo, $79.99–$95.99/yr (legacy SKUs exist). Apple Health/Watch/Strava/Fitbit. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fitbod-gym-fitness-planner/id1041517543
- REPORTED (Sensor Tower ~March 2026): ~200K downloads/mo, ~$2M revenue/mo (~$24M/yr gross run rate). Growjo $7.9M conflicts — weaker methodology; prefer Sensor Tower.
- Category context REPORTED: health & fitness IAP $4.5B in 2025, +13% YoY (Sensor Tower).
- INFERRED: Fitbod ~30–130x larger than every adaptive-programming competitor by ratings volume; category leader for "app generates my workout"; 40+/injury-aware slice unowned.

## Cross-Cutting Takeaways (INFERRED)
1. Pricing bands: loggers $30/yr (Strong) → generators $60–96/yr (Boostcamp, Fitbod, Alpha, FitnessAI) → "AI coach" premium $350–400/yr (Juggernaut, Dr. Muscle). MastersFit's $49.99/yr sits at the bottom of the generator band — arguably underpriced for a coach-tier product.
2. Injury/limitation accommodation is the whitespace: none of the seven surfaces it as a first-class programming input; best-in-class is Juggernaut's readiness check-in (fatigue, not injury).
3. Age-aware programming does not exist in-product anywhere — only Dr. Muscle's SEO funnel; 40+ niche leader position vacant at scale.
4. "AI" labels are mostly progression math; a genuinely LLM-generated, constraint-aware weekly program is still a differentiator — burden is proving coherence/safety (FitnessAI abandonment and Dr. Muscle UX complaints show failure modes).
