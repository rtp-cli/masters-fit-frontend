Note: Healthspan/WHOOP Age is gated to the **Peak tier and above**, not the base tier.
- **Pricing (subscription-only)**: WHOOP One $199/yr (core sleep/strain/recovery); Peak $239/yr (adds Healthspan/WHOOP Age, stress, Health Monitor); Life $359/yr (ECG, AFib detection, BP insights beta, MG hardware). — REPORTED (https://www.whoop.com/us/en/membership/ returned 403 to fetch; pricing corroborated by https://trackervs.com/pricing/whoop-pricing/ and https://www.whoop.com/us/en/thelocker/which-membership-is-right-for-you/ search snippets).
- **Scale**: $575M Series G at $10.1B valuation closed March 2026; ~2.5M members; bookings +103% YoY, ~$1.1B run rate. — REPORTED (https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/31/whoop-valuation-10b-series-g-fundraise/; https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/wearable-fitness-tech-ai-whoop-seriesg-funding/).
- **Does it generate strength programs? YES — increasingly.** WHOOP Coach (OpenAI-powered, launched 2023) produces training plans; a Feb 2026 update lets Strength Trainer build complete structured workouts (exercises/sets/reps/suggested weights) from text prompts or screenshots, factoring lifting history and Recovery score. This makes WHOOP a partial competitor to MastersFit, not just an ecosystem. — REPORTED (https://gadgetsandwearables.com/2026/02/22/whoop-ai-beta/; https://www.wareable.com/fitness-trackers/whoop-coach-ai-strength-trainer-workout-builder-update; https://support.whoop.com/s/article/How-to-Use-the-AI-Powered-WHOOP-Coach). - **API access: YES.** WHOOP Developer Platform exposes recovery, sleep, strain, and workout data via OAuth 2.0 REST API; app registration + approval process + brand guidelines; pricing of production access not stated on docs page. WHOOP also syncs to Apple Health. — CONFIRMED (fetched https://developer.whoop.com/docs/introduction/, 2026-07-30).

- **API access: YES.** Samsung Health Data SDK (the old Samsung Health SDK for Android was deprecated July 31, 2025) exposes sleep/exercise/BP/glucose/food with user consent; Samsung Health also reads/writes **Health Connect**, the simplest integration path. — REPORTED (https://developer.samsung.com/health/blog/en/accessing-samsung-health-data-through-health-connect; https://www.samsungmobilepress.com/articles/samsungs-new-health-software-development-kit-suite-powers-advancements-in-healthcare-innovation).

**Section takeaway (INFERRED):** Every major ecosystem now tracks/scores longevity metrics, and two (WHOOP, Google/Fitbit) have crossed into generating strength workouts — but none is purpose-built for 40+ bodies, injury/surgery history, or progressive strength programming as the product core. All of them are readable via HealthKit/Health Connect, which MastersFit already integrates; Oura's partner program and WHOOP's developer platform are the two feasible direct-integration/co-marketing targets.

## 4. Longevity Market — size, funding, durability
- **Market size**: "Longevity market" estimates vary wildly by definition — one mid-range analyst house puts it at $32.15B (2025) → $34.57B (2026) → $66.4B by 2035 (7.5% CAGR); promotional/VC framings claim "$8 trillion by 2030" (healthspan-economy definition). Treat the $8T number as narrative, not addressable market. — REPORTED (https://www.marketresearchfuture.com/reports/longevity-market-42067; https://www.entrepreneur.com/leadership/this-is-the-8-trillion-opportunity-vcs-and-founders-cant/502671) / INFERRED caveat.
- **Funding**: longevity investment roughly doubled in 2024 to ~$8.5B across ~325 deals; 2025 milestones include Retro Biosciences' $1B Series A and Eli Lilly's first longevity investment; Khosla Ventures the most active VC (10+ deals 2022-2025). — REPORTED (https://altstreet.investments/blog/longevity-funding-landscape-2026-geroscience-investment; https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/longevity-funding-analysis).
- **Consumer-side proof points (stronger evidence of durability than biotech)**: Oura $11B, WHOOP $10.1B, Function $2.5B, all raised/re-rated within the last ~12 months (sources above). Consumer "healthspan" spend is where the money actually cleared — INFERRED.
- **Skeptical takes (real and citable)**: MIT Technology Review documented longevity clinics selling unproven treatments (Apr 2025) — https://www.technologyreview.com/2025/04/18/1115372/longevity-clinics-selling-unproven-treatments/; Forbes (Jun 2026) on biological-age tests: no standardization, different "clocks" give the same person different ages — https://www.forbes.com/sites/juergeneckhardt/2026/06/23/what-to-know-about-tests-that-promise-to-reveal-your-biological-age/; Unity Biotechnology (flagship senolytics firm) shuttered Sept 2025 after a failed trial — REPORTED (https://lifespan.io/longevity-biotech-in-2025-the-expert-roundup/). — all REPORTED.
- **Durability verdict (INFERRED)**: the *pharmacological* longevity story is hype-prone; but exercise — especially strength training — is the single intervention every credible skeptic endorses. "Exercise layer of longevity" is the defensible, evidence-backed corner of a frothy category; MastersFit should cite exercise-evidence, not biological-age clocks.

## 6. Trust / Regulatory
- **FDA**: finalized **"General Wellness: Policy for Low Risk Devices" on Jan 6, 2026** (supersedes 2019), keeping the two-factor test: (1) general-wellness intended use only, (2) low risk. Two allowed claim categories: pure wellness claims (fitness, sleep, weight) and **disease-referenced claims tied to healthy lifestyle** where the lifestyle-risk link is well understood ("living well with" / "may reduce risk of"). 2026 update even loosens treatment of some noninvasive physiological measurements. FDA held a town hall Feb 11, 2026. — REPORTED (https://www.cov.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2026/01/fda-issues-revised-guidance-on-general-wellness-products; https://www.troutman.com/insights/fdas-2026-guidance-on-general-wellness-devices-policy-for-low-risk-devices/; FDA town-hall page https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/medical-devices-news-and-events/town-hall-general-wellness-policy-low-risk-devices-final-guidance-02112026).
- **Implication for MastersFit's injury/surgery accommodation (INFERRED, consistent with counsel commentary)**: accommodating a user-reported injury history in workout selection stays inside general wellness *so long as* the app doesn't claim to diagnose, treat, or rehabilitate a condition. Safe framing: "works around your limitations"; risky framing: "rehabs your shoulder," "safe after surgery," "reduces injury" as a proven outcome.
- **FTC**: general-wellness status does **not** exempt advertising. The FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance (Dec 2022, replacing 1998 guide) requires "competent and reliable scientific evidence" for health benefit claims; "clinically proven / doctor-recommended" trigger heightened scrutiny needing clinical-level substantiation. — REPORTED (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance; https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2023/2023-03-02-ftc-revises-health-products-compliance-guidance).
- **How incumbents handle it**:
  - **Peloton** (ToS §17 Safety Warnings): "YOU SHOULD CONSULT YOUR PHYSICIAN… BEFORE BEGINNING A NEW FITNESS PROGRAM"; "NOT MEDICAL OR HEALTHCARE ADVICE"; service "DESIGNED FOR EDUCATIONAL AND ENTERTAINMENT PURPOSES ONLY"; plus an affirmative user representation of no heart condition/chest pain/balance issues or physician approval. — CONFIRMED (fetched https://www.onepeloton.com/terms-of-service, 2026-07-30).
  - **Fitbod**: in-app "Injuries and Limitations" feature lets users flag body areas; app excludes/substitutes exercises and offers "Mark As Not Injured" to restore; help docs state suggestions are not medical advice and users should consult a qualified healthcare professional before exercising with an injury. — REPORTED (https://help.fitbod.me/hc/en-us/articles/37629269518103-Injuries-and-Limitations — direct fetch 403'd; content via search snippets + https://fitbod.me/terms-conditions/).
  - **Future**: relies on human coaches adapting programming around injuries (human-in-the-loop rather than algorithmic disclaiming). — REPORTED (https://www.trainwell.net/blog/the-best-personalized-fitness-apps-that-pair-you-with-a-coach) / partially INFERRED.
- **Practical checklist for MastersFit (INFERRED from the above)**: keep the WHOOP-style "for wellness purposes only, not medical use" line on any longevity metric; physician-consult gate at onboarding when injuries/surgery are declared (Peloton-style affirmative representation); avoid "rehab/recover/treat" verbs in UI and ASO copy; substantiate any "build strength / improve healthspan" claims with citations to exercise literature, not app-specific outcomes, until you have data.

## Cross-cutting strategic notes (INFERRED)
1. **Competitive clock is ticking**: Google's Gemini coach ($9.99/mo, GA May 19 2026) and WHOOP's AI Strength Trainer both now generate strength workouts and adjust for injuries — MastersFit's moat must be 40+ specificity (injury/surgery depth, age-calibrated progression), not "AI generates workouts."
2. **Apple's retreat is the opening**: Health+ shelved as a standalone; iOS has no first-party adaptive strength coach for now. App Store Featuring Nomination tied to a longevity-angle release is cheap, immediate distribution.
3. **Reading everyone's data is easy; being read is the play**: HealthKit + Health Connect (already integrated) cover Apple/Samsung/Fitbit/Oura/Garmin passively; direct badges via Oura Partner Integrations and the WHOOP Developer Platform are the two attainable co-marketing integrations.
4. **Labs platforms are complementary, not competitive**: Function (200K members), Superpower, InsideTracker all prescribe "do strength training" with no product to fulfill it — the cleanest partnership wedge for "the exercise layer."
5. **Distribution ladder for a small DTC app**: HSA/FSA eligibility (Truemed) → Wellhub Partner Apps → UHC Store / One Pass Select Digital tier → insurer wellbeing ecosystems (Sworkit precedent). Employer/insurer channels are slow but proven accessible to small apps.
