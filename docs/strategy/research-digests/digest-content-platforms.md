# Competitive Research Digest — MastersFit vs. Major Fitness Platforms

**Verification date: 2026-07-30.** Labels: **CONFIRMED** = first-party page fetched today; **REPORTED** = credible secondary source; **INFERRED** = analyst judgment from confirmed facts; **UNAVAILABLE** = could not verify.

---

## 1. Apple Fitness+

### Pricing — CONFIRMED
- $9.99/month or $79.99/year; 1 month free trial (3 months with eligible device purchase); included in Apple One Premier bundle. No free tier beyond trial.
- Source: https://www.apple.com/apple-fitness-plus/

### App Store rating — CONFIRMED (with caveat)
- Fitness+ lives inside Apple's built-in **Fitness** app: **2.9/5, ~11K ratings** (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fitness/id1208224953). Caveat (INFERRED): this rating covers the whole Fitness app (rings/tracking complaints), not Fitness+ content satisfaction specifically; pre-installed Apple apps get rated differently than downloaded apps.

### Workout creation — CONFIRMED
- Curated **trainer-led video library**: 12 workout types, 5–45 min, new sessions weekly. "Custom Plans" build a *schedule* from your favorite activities/trainers/durations — it sequences existing videos; it does not generate or adapt exercise programming. No AI generation.
- Source: apple.com/apple-fitness-plus/ + App Store listing.

### Personalization inputs — CONFIRMED / INFERRED
- Inputs: activity preferences, trainer, duration, music, equipment filters (CONFIRMED). **No injury/limitation, schedule-constraint, or age inputs** (INFERRED from absence on official pages; workouts are one-size video content with optional on-screen modifier trainer).

### Age-aware content — CONFIRMED
- **"Workouts for Older Adults"** program (launched April 19, 2021): strength, core, yoga, dance targeting age-related strength/flexibility/balance/mobility; chair-support option, one light dumbbell; modifications usable in any studio workout.
- Sources: https://fitness.apple.com/ca/program/workouts-for-older-adults/1562415709 ; https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2021/04/apple-fitness-plus-introduces-new-workouts-trainers-and-time-to-walk-guest/
- Community threads show users complaining the older-adults catalog is thin/stale (REPORTED: discussions.apple.com threads, e.g. "Why is there no 30 day chair workouts for older adults?").

### Health/wearable integration — CONFIRMED
- Deepest in class on iOS: Apple Watch real-time HR/rings on screen, AirPods/Bluetooth HR monitors, iPhone/iPad/Apple TV. **iOS-only** — no Android (CONFIRMED by device requirements: iPhone 8+/iOS 16.1+).

### Scale — UNAVAILABLE (first-party); REPORTED estimates
- Apple does not disclose Fitness+ subscribers. Market-share estimates ~15–17% of US fitness-app market (REPORTED: businessofapps.com / wifitalents.com, 2026). Treat as directional only.

### Vs. MastersFit (INFERRED)
- **Strengths:** price ($9.99 is the floor of the paid set), production quality, Watch integration, an explicit older-adults program, Apple distribution.
- **Weaknesses:** zero true program generation — cannot adapt around injuries, equipment beyond filters, or progressive overload; older-adults content is a small static program, criticized as under-refreshed; iOS-only; no strength progression tracking by weight/reps.

## 2. Peloton App

### Pricing — CONFIRMED
- **App One: $15.99/mo or $159/yr; App+: $28.99/mo or $289/yr** (App Store in-app purchase list, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/peloton-fitness-workouts/id792750948). 30-day free trial on both; **Strength+ $9.99/mo** (14-day trial) as a separate gym-companion app (https://www.onepeloton.com/membership — note: this page renders prices via JS; App Store listing used for exact figures). There is a limited free tier ("App Free") — REPORTED/legacy; the current membership page pushes trials, not the free tier.

### App Store rating — CONFIRMED
- **4.9/5, ~814K ratings**, Editors' Choice. Largest rating volume in this set by ~3x.

### Workout creation — CONFIRMED (first-party)
- Core = massive live + on-demand **class library** (15+ types, 5–90 min). **Peloton IQ** (launched Oct 1, 2025) is genuine movement toward generation/adaptation: "personal, weekly workout roadmaps," recommendations that "become sharper" with every workout, computer-vision rep counting, real-time form feedback, weight recommendations (https://www.onepeloton.com/peloton-iq).
- **Key nuance (CONFIRMED):** full IQ (rep tracking, form feedback, *workout generator*, weight suggestions) requires **premium hardware** (Bike+/Tread+/Row+ with camera) + All-Access Membership. App-only members get Personalized Plans/recommendations — still assembling *existing classes* into a schedule, not generating bespoke exercise prescriptions.
- Personalized Plans adoption: 800K+ members started one (REPORTED: Peloton press release, https://investor.onepeloton.com/news-releases/news-release-details/peloton-enters-new-era-ai-powered-peloton-iq-and-new-product).

### Personalization inputs — CONFIRMED
- Goals (incl. explicitly **"Longevity"**, "Get Stronger", weight goals), activity preferences, frequency, duration, experience level, workout history, target muscle groups. **No injury/limitation input** ("does not explicitly mention injuries or physical limitations as system inputs" — onepeloton.com/peloton-iq).

### Age-aware / longevity positioning — CONFIRMED + REPORTED
- "Longevity" is a first-class plan goal in Peloton IQ (CONFIRMED). Strength push: 2M members actively strength training as of Q4 FY2025 (REPORTED: Peloton press release via businesswire). No senior-specific content line; positioning is longevity-for-everyone, skewing 30s–50s (INFERRED).

### Health/wearable integration — CONFIRMED
- Apple Watch integration (App Store listing); syncs with Apple Health; Strava/Garmin ecosystem well established (REPORTED, long-standing).

### Scale — REPORTED (company filings, Q3 FY2026, quarter ended 2026-03-31)
- **2.662M paid Connected Fitness subs (−7.6% YoY); 522K paid app subs (declining)**; churn 1.2%/mo. Sources: https://investor.onepeloton.com/news-releases/news-release-details/peloton-announces-q3-fy2026-financial-results ; https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/07/peloton-pton-earnings-q3-2026.html

### Vs. MastersFit (INFERRED)
- **Strengths:** the most serious AI/personalization investment among these five; huge brand, community, class production; explicit longevity framing overlaps MastersFit's wedge.
- **Weaknesses:** best AI features gated behind $2–3K camera hardware + All-Access; app-tier subscriber base is *shrinking*; no injury/limitation intake — an adult with a bad shoulder still gets pointed at classes, not a regenerated program; instructor-video format can't substitute exercises mid-plan the way a generator can.

## 3. Nike Training Club

### Pricing — CONFIRMED
- **Free.** No subscription; App Store listing shows no in-app purchases (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nike-training-club-wellness/id301521403). (Premium tier was eliminated and all content made free in 2020 — REPORTED, widely documented.)

### App Store rating — CONFIRMED
- **4.8/5, ~280K ratings.**

### Workout creation — CONFIRMED
- Trainer-led VOD **library** + fixed "monthlong structured programs." No generation, no adaptation, no AI. It's free content marketing for Nike.

### Personalization inputs — CONFIRMED (thin)
- Fitness-level-based program selection; gym vs. home setting. No injuries, no schedule builder, no age input visible on listing (INFERRED from absence).

### Age-aware content — UNAVAILABLE
- No older-adult or longevity-specific programming found on first-party surfaces. Positioning is athletic/performance (INFERRED).

### Health/wearable integration — CONFIRMED
- Apple Watch app (control workout, HR/calories on wrist), Apple Health sync, Nike Run Club cross-logging.

### Scale — UNAVAILABLE
- Nike doesn't report NTC MAUs. Download counts are large but stale/unverifiable.

### Vs. MastersFit (INFERRED)
- **Strengths:** free — it's the "why pay?" anchor; strong brand; decent library.
- **Weaknesses:** zero personalization depth, zero adaptation, no 40+ positioning; as a free static library it competes on price, not on the job MastersFit does (programming around limitations). Least direct threat of the five.

## 4. Centr

### Pricing — CONFIRMED (via App Store IAP list; centr.com hides prices behind quiz)
- In-app subscriptions: **monthly $15.99–$29.99; annual $119.99–$152.99** (tier ladder), **7-day free trial**; 15% off annual via FitQuiz; FSA/HSA accepted. Sources: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/centr-strength-fitness-app/id1382530817 ; https://www.centr.com/join-us
- Note (INFERRED): the range likely reflects promo vs. standard tiers; headline price is toward the top of the range.

### App Store rating — CONFIRMED
- **4.8/5, ~21K ratings.** Smallest rating base of the five.

### Workout creation — CONFIRMED
- Trainer-built **programs** (12 named coaches incl. Luke Zocchi; HYROX-certified program) assigned via a **FitQuiz**: "design a plan just for you," programming "reps, weight, and intensity to match your level" (beginner/intermediate/advanced). This is quiz-routed templating, **not AI generation** — the plan is selected/parameterized, not composed per user (CONFIRMED language + INFERRED mechanism).

### Personalization inputs — CONFIRMED (moderate)
- Goals, preferences, skill level, exercise/dietary preferences; meal plans included. **No injury/limitation intake, no age input** found (INFERRED from absence).

### Age-aware content — PARTIAL
- "Centr Begin" 3-week **low-impact beginner program** (CONFIRMED, centr.com/join-us). No age-specific or 40+/longevity positioning; brand is Hemsworth/aspirational-physique (INFERRED).

### Health/wearable integration — CONFIRMED
- Apple Watch real-time tracking, WearOS app, TV casting.

### Scale — REPORTED (stale)
- **200K+ subscribers at March 2022 acquisition** by HighPost Capital (~$200M deal); HighPost projected 700K in four years — projection, not result. Sources: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220330005951/en/ ; cinemablend.com. Current figure UNAVAILABLE.

### Vs. MastersFit (INFERRED)
- **Strengths:** holistic bundle (training + meals + mindfulness), celebrity reach, quiz-based onboarding is the closest UX analog to MastersFit's intake.
- **Weaknesses:** template plans can't adapt week-to-week or around limitations; young/aesthetic brand actively mismatches 40+ users; small installed base; priciest tier rivals Peloton App+ without the AI.

## 5. iFIT

### Pricing — CONFIRMED
- **iFIT Train: $14.99/mo or $143.99/yr** (1 user, phone/tablet + Bluetooth equipment); **iFIT Pro: $39.99/mo or $394.99/yr** (up to 5 users, touchscreen NordicTrack/ProForm/Freemotion machines). App-store copy references a 30-day free trial for non-equipment users (App Store listing). Note: ifit.com/membership showed $15/$39 rounded. Sources: https://www.ifit.com/membership ; https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ifit-workouts/id1274601042

### App Store rating — CONFIRMED
- **4.7/5, ~71K ratings** (iFIT Workouts, id1274601042).

### Workout creation — CONFIRMED
- 10,000+ workout **library** (180 trainers, scenic destination content) + genuine **adaptation layer**: **SmartAdjust** ("automatically adapts your machine's speed, incline, or resistance to suit your fitness level"), **ActivePulse** HR-based auto-adjust (treadmills), "AI-powered workout recommendations tailored to your fitness goals and schedule," and an "iFIT AI Coach" chat for goal setting. Adaptation is **machine-parameter-level** (speed/incline/resistance), not program-composition-level (INFERRED distinction).

### Personalization inputs — CONFIRMED (equipment-centric)
- Fitness level (inferred from performance), HR zones, goals/schedule for recommendations. **No injury/limitation or age intake** found (INFERRED from absence). Pro tier has a Workout Creator (manual).

### Age-aware content — UNAVAILABLE
- No older-adult program or longevity positioning found on first-party pages. Cardio-equipment DNA; strength is secondary (INFERRED).

### Health/wearable integration — CONFIRMED
- Apple Health, Google Fit, Garmin Connect, Strava sync; Apple Watch companion app; Bluetooth HR monitors.

### Scale — REPORTED
- **6M+ members** (April 2025, athletechnews.com; reiterated Dec 2025, forbes.com). Historic paid-subscriber baseline: 600K+ paid of 3.3M members in March 2020 (sgbonline.com) — current *paid* split UNAVAILABLE. Sources: https://athletechnews.com/ifit-subscriber-growth-ai-tools-gamified-content/ ; https://www.forbes.com/sites/pamdanziger/2025/12/30/ifit-ramps-up-fitness-offerings-to-help-people-keep-their-new-years-resolutions/

### Vs. MastersFit (INFERRED)
- **Strengths:** real closed-loop adaptation (the only one here that changes the workout *during* the workout), big member base, hardware moat, multi-platform health sync.
- **Weaknesses:** value is chained to owning NordicTrack-family cardio equipment; adaptation tunes machine resistance, not exercise selection or joint-friendly substitutions; no limitation-aware programming; strength training and 40+ positioning are afterthoughts.

## Cross-cutting synthesis (INFERRED)

| | Price/mo | Rating (vol) | True generation? | Injury/limitation input? | 40+ positioning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Apple Fitness+ | $9.99 | 2.9 (11K)* | No — schedules videos | No | Yes (small static program) |
| Peloton App | $15.99–28.99 | 4.9 (814K) | Partial — IQ, best parts hardware-gated | No | "Longevity" goal, not age-specific |
| Nike TC | Free | 4.8 (280K) | No | No | None |
| Centr | ~$15.99–29.99 | 4.8 (21K) | No — quiz→template | No | None (low-impact beginner program only) |
| iFIT | $14.99–39.99 | 4.7 (71K) | Partial — machine-parameter adaptation | No | None |

\* rating is for the whole Apple Fitness app, not Fitness+ content.

**The gap MastersFit sits in:** none of the five collects injuries/physical limitations as a programming input, and none composes a program per user from an exercise level up — Peloton IQ is closest but gates its generator behind premium hardware and still starts from a class library. Age-awareness is either a static side-program (Apple) or a goal label (Peloton "Longevity"). The competitive threats to watch are (1) Peloton IQ trickling generator features down to the $15.99 app tier, and (2) Apple expanding Custom Plans with health-data-driven adaptation. The durable differentiator to press: limitation-aware generation + regeneration (equipment, time budget, injuries) — the exact thing a video library structurally cannot do.
